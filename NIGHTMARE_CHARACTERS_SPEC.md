# Nightmare Characters (CH-037..CH-043) — Spec

## CH-037 Josh Stone (maxHp 80, L1@0, L2@7)
- L1: When healed for the first time during your turn, +20 gold.
- L2: At beginning of turn, if at max HP, may move one weapon from discard to hand.

## CH-038 Mikhail Victor (maxHp 80, L1@0, L2@5)
- L1: Weapons cost 10 less to buy.
- L2: While using weapons with different weapon types, +10 damage.

## CH-039 Carlos Oliveira (maxHp 80, L1@4, L2@9)
- L1: At beginning of turn, +20 gold.
- L2: When you buy a card costing 80+, +1 explore.

## CH-040 Mysterious Mask (maxHp 80, L1@4, L2@8)
- L1: When you defeat an infected, may trash a card from discard.
- L2: During turn, may discard one action card from hand for +2 cards.

## CH-041 Albert Wesker (maxHp 110, L1@1, L2@6)
- L1: At beginning of turn, if only one other player's character has <=30 HP, may +1 card.
- L2: During turn, may choose that player to discard one card.

## CH-042 Leon S. Kennedy (maxHp 80, L1@3, L2@6)
- L1: When Leon defeats an infected, may trash a card from discard; if so, gain a card costing up to 20 more than trashed.
- L2: When you gain a card, +1 card.

## CH-043 Ada Wong (maxHp 70, L1@1, L2@10)
- L1: During turn, if Ada takes damage, +2 cards.
- L2: When Ada's HP would be reduced to 0 or less, set to 10 instead.

Open questions per character will be asked before implementation.
