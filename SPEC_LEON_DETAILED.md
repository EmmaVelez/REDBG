# Leon S. Kennedy (CH-042) Detailed Spec

## L1: trashCardFromDiscardGainCardCostingUpTo20MoreOnInfectedDefeat (level >= 3)
- Trigger: on defeat of an infected by this character's player.
- Effect: prompt to trash one card from discard pile; if trashed, gain a card from market costing up to (trashed card cost + 20).

## L2: plusOneCardOnGainCard (level >= 6)
- Trigger: whenever the player gains a card (buy or effect).
- Effect: +1 card (draw one).
