# Mysterious Mask (CH-040) Detailed Spec

## L1: trashCardFromDiscardOnInfectedDefeat (level >= 4)
- Trigger: on defeat of an infected by this character's player.
- Effect: prompt to trash one card from discard pile (optional). On confirm, trash selected card.

## L2: discardActionCardFromHandForPlusTwoCards (level >= 8)
- Trigger: during player's turn (action phase).
- Effect: prompt to discard one action card from hand; on confirm, draw +2 cards.
